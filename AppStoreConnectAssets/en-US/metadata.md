## Description

Grateful Moments Journal is a private gratitude diary for saving the small moments that made your day better.

Add a title, note, and optional photo to each moment. Your entries become a growing path of hexagon cards, while streaks and achievement badges help turn reflection into a gentle daily habit.

Key features:
- Save gratitude moments with a title, note, and photo
- Track your journaling streak
- Unlock achievement badges as you keep going
- Export your journal to PDF or CSV
- Reflect on saved moments with the Assistant on supported Apple Intelligence devices
- Keep entries stored on your device
- Use the app with no ads

You can create up to 30 moments for free. Premium unlocks unlimited moments, PDF and CSV export, and the Assistant on supported devices.

Your entries and photos are stored on your device. The developer cannot view your journal content, and export only happens when you explicitly choose it. Grateful Moments Journal is made for people who want a calm, private way to notice and keep everyday gratitude.
