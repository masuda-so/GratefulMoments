Small Thanks Diary

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Small Thanks Diary handles your information.

Data Collection

Small Thanks Diary does not collect personal data from the app.

Your journal entries, notes, photos, streaks, badges, and app data are stored on your device. The developer does not operate a server for collecting or viewing this content.

Photos

If you add a photo to a journal entry, the photo is used only for the entry you create. Photos remain on your device as part of the app data.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment card information.

Export

PDF and CSV export happens only when you choose to export your data. Exported files are created on your device and are controlled by you.

Analytics and Advertising

Small Thanks Diary does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or need app support, contact so.masuda.2003@pm.me.
