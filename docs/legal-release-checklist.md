# Legal Release Checklist

> This document is an engineering compliance checklist, not legal advice.
> Before public release, paid distribution, user-data collection, or production operation, confirm the final position with a qualified professional.


## Current Position

小さなありがとう日記 (Grateful Moments Journal) is a local-first gratitude journal with photos, Apple Intelligence-assisted reflection, Premium subscriptions, and PDF/CSV export.

## Checks

- Privacy Policy states that the developer does not collect journal entries, notes, photos, streaks, badges, or app data.
- App Store privacy labels match the app and policy.
- Assistant behavior remains on device unless clearly disclosed otherwise.
- Premium subscription terms, restore purchase, cancellation, and Apple standard EULA links are visible.
- Exported PDF/CSV files are user-controlled and may contain private journal data.
- Support channels warn users not to include private journal entries, photos, passwords, or payment details.

## Recommended Additions

- Japanese Privacy Policy.
- Japanese support/privacy links if Japan is a target market.
- In-app export warning for files leaving app-controlled storage.
