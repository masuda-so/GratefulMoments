## Before Contacting Support

Please include:

- App version
- iOS version
- Device model
- A short description of what you expected to happen
- A short description of what happened instead

Do not include private journal entries, photos, passwords, or payment details in support messages.

## Premium

Premium subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. Purchases can be restored from the Premium screen in the app. The developer cannot view or manage your Apple ID payment method.
